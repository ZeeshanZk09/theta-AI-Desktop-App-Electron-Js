# AI Engineer Rapid Offer Sprint

## 1) Market Angle
*   **Target Audience:** SMBs with specific bottlenecks (lead qualification, data entry, customer support).
*   **Positioning:** Low-commitment, high-impact 7-day pilot project, appealing in uncertain markets.

## 2) 7-Day Execution Steps
*   **Day 1:** Define single high-value outcome (e.g., Automated Lead Qualification System).
*   **Day 2-3:** Package offer and set premium pricing for profitability.
*   **Day 4-5:** Create Minimum Viable Product collateral (Landing Page, Proposal).
*   **Day 6:** Build warm contacts list.
*   **Day 7:** Pre-Sale and validation.

## 3) Key Risks
*   Scope Creep (major risk, undermines "Rapid" value).
*   Under-delivery within 7 days.

## 4) Expected ROI Assumptions
*   High conversion rate due to trust with warm contacts.
*   Quick revenue generation through premium pricing and focused offer.
