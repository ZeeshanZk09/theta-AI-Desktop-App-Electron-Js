# 🤖 Theta AI - Voice Assistant Capabilities

**Aapki JARVIS-Level AI Assistant**

---

## 📋 **Quick Overview**

Theta ek powerful voice-controlled AI assistant hai jo aapke desktop par kaam karta hai. Yeh dekh sakta hai (camera + screen), samajh sakta hai (PDFs, images, documents), aur real actions perform kar sakta hai (files banaye, apps open kare, WhatsApp message bheje).

---

## 🎯 **Core Capabilities** (Kya-Kya Kar Sakta Hai)

### 🌐 **1. Knowledge & Search**

- **Google Search Integration**: Real-time web search kar ke latest information de sakta hai
- **News & Weather Updates**: Dashboard par latest headlines aur mausam ki jankari update kare
- **Real-time Information**: Currency rates, stock prices, ya koi bhi online data fetch kare

**Use Cases:**

- "Aaj ke top headlines kya hain?"
- "Bitcoin ka price kya hai?"
- "Lahore mein mausam kaisa hai?"

---

### 👁️ **2. Visual Intelligence** (AI Dekh Sakta Hai!)

- **Live Camera Feed**: 1 FPS par aapka camera dekh sakta hai (JARVIS ki tarah)
- **Screen Capture**: Apke screen ko capture karke analyze kar sakta hai
- **File Analysis**: Images, PDFs, Documents (Word/Excel), aur text files ko read kar sakta hai

**Use Cases:**

- "Meri screen dekh ke bata yeh error kya hai?"
- "Is PDF ko read karke summarize kar do"
- "Yeh image mein kya likha hai?" (OCR)
- [Camera on karke] "Mere saamne kya hai?"

### 🎨 **3. Creative Tools**

- **AI Image Generation**: Text se images bana sakta hai aur automatically download folder mein save kare
- **Diagram Creation**: Flowcharts, mindmaps, sequence diagrams (Mermaid.js) bana sakta hai concepts explain karne ke liye
- **Visual Explanations**: Complex topics ko visual diagrams se samjha sakta hai

**Use Cases:**

- "Ek futuristic city ki image banao"

**Use Cases:**

- "Zeeshan ko WhatsApp par message bhejo: Meeting 5 PM par hai"

**Use Cases:**

### 🧠 **9. Intelligent Problem Solving**

Theta ek **JARVIS-level mindset** ke saath kaam karta hai:
