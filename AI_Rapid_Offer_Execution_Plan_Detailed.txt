### Detailed Execution Plan: AI Rapid Offer Sprint (7 Days)
**Focus:** Basic Customer Support Automation (AI-Powered FAQ Bot/Instant Response System)

---

#### Phase 1: Day 1 - Strategic Definition & Scoping (Complete)
*   **Objective:** Finalize MVP scope that balances rapid delivery (7 days) with high market value.
*   **Selected MVP:** AI-Powered Instant Customer Support Bot.
*   **Key Deliverable:** Detailed Scope Document confirming feasible feature set for the sprint duration.

---

#### Phase 2: Day 2-3 - Rapid Development & Feature Implementation
*   **Objective:** Build the core Minimum Viable Product (MVP).
*   **Technical Workflow:** Setup development environment, select low-code/no-code AI platform or framework for rapid prototyping.
*   **Detailed Features to Include (MVP Scope):**
    *   **Instant Canned Responses:** Pre-defined, AI-suggested responses for common queries to ensure immediate customer interaction.
    *   **Dynamic FAQ Integration:** A knowledge base driven FAQ system that uses Natural Language Understanding (NLU) to interpret user questions and provide precise answers.
    *   **Basic Lead Capture:** A critical feature where the bot identifies unanswered queries or high-intent phrases and captures user contact information (Name, Email, Phone) for human follow-up.
    *   **Simple Hand-off Mechanism:** A clear path for the user to request human intervention if the bot cannot resolve the query.

---

#### Phase 3: Day 4-5 - Testing, Refinement & Collateral Creation
*   **Objective:** Validate functionality and prepare necessary sales materials.
*   **Testing:**
    *   Internal Scenario Testing: Ensure all defined features (FAQ, Instant Response, Lead Capture) work flawlessly.
    *   User Acceptance Testing (UAT): Limited testing with internal stakeholders to gather quick feedback.
*   **Material Creation:**
    *   Minimum Viable Landing Page: A focused page highlighting the "Rapid Offer" value proposition (24/7 support, instant response, time-saving).
    *   Proposal Template: A standardized, compelling proposal for validation pitches.

---

#### Phase 4: Day 6-7 - Outreach & Validation
*   **Objective:** Test market acceptance and gather real-world feedback.
*   **Strategy:** Execute the planned outreach strategy by pitching the packaged solution to warm contacts.
*   **Deliverables:**
    *   Validated Offer: Confirmation of market need and price acceptance.
    *   Feedback Analysis: Document feedback to refine the product or pivot the strategy for subsequent sprints.

---
This detailed plan ensures a structured approach to achieve a high-value, deployable product within the 7-day timeframe.
